T² measures movement inside the healthy correlation structure; SPE measures movement orthogonal to it — a new relationship between channels that the healthy engine never showed. Contributions decompose the score per feature, so the alert names its own evidence.
Fault isolation — ranked hypotheses
Structured-residual isolation: each candidate fault has a prescribed pattern of symptoms it must produce and symptoms it must not. Evidence listed against a hypothesis lowers its confidence.

State estimator — normalised innovations
StateMeasuredEstimate±σInnov (σ)Bias (σ)
On a healthy engine every innovation is zero-mean and about one sigma. A persistently biased innovation on one channel while the rest stay clean is an instrument fault, not an engine fault.
Fault injection (demonstration)
Injected faults perturb the simulated engine only. The twin has no knowledge of what was injected — everything it reports is inferred from the CAN signals alone.
